The **🎧 Voice support** module creates a voice help corner: members wait in silence, staff move them into a private support room to help them out loud (screen share included).

## Setup

1. **Category**: pick where the channels go. Kron suggests your tickets category.
2. **Who can wait**: leave empty for everyone, or pick roles.
3. **Support staff**: the roles that see the support rooms and move members.
4. Adjust the names (emoji, separator, font) and the **number of support rooms** (1 to 10).
5. **Save**: Kron creates `🔊┆Support 1`, `🔊┆Support 2`… and `🔊┆Waiting room` under them.

Saving again updates the channels (names, category, access). A room removed from the list is deleted.

## Waiting room

- **Total silence**: members cannot talk, share their screen, or post text, GIFs, emojis or reactions in the voice chat.
- **Deafen** (on by default): Kron deafens waiting members and gives the sound back as soon as they are moved.
- Staff can talk there and move members.

## Support rooms

- Visible **to staff only**.
- Staff move a member from the waiting room into a room (right click > Move To, or drag).
- In the room, the member can **talk without push-to-talk** and **share their screen**. They lose access when they leave.

> [!TIP]
> Kron needs the Manage Channels, Manage Roles, Move Members, Mute Members and Deafen Members permissions.
