The **Channel builder** shows your server's tree (categories and channels) and lets you rearrange everything from the dashboard. Nothing is sent to Discord before you confirm.

## Editing

- **Drag** a channel to move it, within its category or into another. Drag a category to move it. The ↑ ↓ arrows do the same from the keyboard.
- **Click a name** to rename it: emoji, **separator** (`┆`, `│`, `・`, `»`…), text and **font** (𝐁𝐨𝐥𝐝, 𝘐𝘵𝘢𝘭𝘪𝘤, 𝗦𝗮𝗻𝘀, 𝙼𝚘𝚗𝚘, 𝔻𝕠𝕦𝕓𝕝𝕖, sᴍᴀʟʟ ᴄᴀᴘs). The preview shows the result.
- **+ Category** at the top, **+ Add a channel** under each category (text, voice, announcement, forum or stage). A new channel copies its category's permissions.
- 🗑️ marks a channel for deletion (struck through in red). A deleted category takes its channels with it. **Restore** undoes it.

> [!NOTE]
> Text channels are always lowercase with dashes: that is a Discord rule.

## Apply

Click **Review and apply** in the bottom bar. The summary lists what will be created, renamed, moved and deleted. Deletions need a confirmation: messages of deleted channels are lost.

Kron applies in order: creations, renames, the new order (in one request), then deletions.

## Server packs

The **📦 Server packs** section adds ready-made categories, channels and roles for your kind of server in one click: **FiveM RP**, **Gaming**, **Streamer** or **Community**.

- The pack joins the tree: anything that already exists with the same name is kept, nothing is deleted.
- The pack's roles are listed under the tree; remove the ones you don't want. A role that already exists (same name) is reused.
- 🔒 categories (staff, services, VIP) are private: only their roles and Kron see them.
- Rename or delete anything, then **Review and apply**. **Cancel** puts everything back.

## Beautify names

**✨ Beautify names** gives every channel the same style: an emoji (kept when there is one, otherwise picked from the name: rules → 📜, tickets → 🎫), a separator and a font. The result shows in the tree before it is applied.

After each apply that renames channels, Kron keeps their previous names: **↩️ Restore previous names** puts them back.

> [!NOTE]
> Discord allows 2 renames per channel every 10 minutes. If you rename and restore too fast, wait a few minutes.
