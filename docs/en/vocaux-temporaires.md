With **temporary voice channels**, a member who joins the **hub** channel gets their own voice channel and is moved into it.

## Setup

1. Create a voice channel, for example `➕ Create a channel`.
2. In the dashboard, pick it as the **hub channel**, and if you want a **category** for new channels.
3. Set the **name** (`{nickname}`, `{username}`, `{count}`) and the number of **slots**.

## What the member can do

The creator manages their channel from Discord: rename it, limit slots, lock it, move members. The channel is **deleted as soon as it is empty**, even if Kron restarted in the meantime.
