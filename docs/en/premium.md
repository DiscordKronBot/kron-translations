Kron's essentials are **free forever**. **Pro** unlocks the advanced modules (FiveM, Twitch, Kick, GitHub, esport) and removes every limit; **Whitelabel** adds your own bot. Every plan is on [kronbot.xyz/pro](/pro).

### If your server is over the free limits

- **Nothing is deleted.** Embeds, forms, ticket panels, commands, settings: everything stays saved.
- What exists past the limit stays in place; you just can't create new items without going Pro.
- Pro modules pause without Pro; their settings stay saved.
- A Premium key keeps its plan until its end date.

## Billing

- Prices are in US dollars. Applicable taxes are added. Kron never sees your card details.
- Monthly and yearly subscriptions **renew automatically**. The 5-year pass is paid once and does not renew.
- **Whitelabel** (your own bot) is an optional add-on on every offer: +$3 USD per month on monthly and yearly, $99 USD in total for the 5-year pass.
- **Moving from monthly to yearly or the 5-year pass**: what you paid in the last 60 days comes off, and the monthly subscription stops on its own.
- Lifetime purchases made before stay lifetime.
- You can **cancel anytime**: access stays until the end of the paid period.
- A price increase is announced **30 days** in advance and only applies to the next period.

## When Pro ends

Your settings are kept. What goes over the Free limits stays visible, but you can't create new items while you are over the limit.
